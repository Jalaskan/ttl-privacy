Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The short version: We collect as little personal data as possible. We don't know your name, email, or Apple ID. But it's important you understand this: lessons you create are public — anyone using the app can find and listen to them through Charts and search, the same way you can find lessons other people have made. This isn't private cloud storage; it's a shared library. This policy explains exactly what that means.

Who we are

Time To Learn ("TTL", "we", "us") is an iOS application. If you have questions about this policy, click here to email us.

What we collect

TTL is designed to work without collecting personal information. Here is everything we do collect:

  • Device identifier (IDFV) — Apple's Identifier for Vendor. A device-level identifier that cannot be linked to your Apple ID or personal identity. Resets if you uninstall. Used for anonymous analytics and to identify which lessons in the shared catalog were created by your device (so we can show you "Your Library").
  • Anonymous usage events — events such as "lesson generated", "app opened", "series completed", and "lesson shared" sent to Mixpanel. These contain no personal identifiers, no lesson audio, and no location data.
  • Subscription status — whether you are on the free, monthly, or annual plan. Managed entirely by Apple via StoreKit 2. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Lessons you create — see "Lessons you create are public" below. This is the most important thing in this policy.
  • Your spoken question, if you use Ask a Question — see "Ask a Question" below.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or Apple ID
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Camera or contacts access
  • Browsing history or cross-app tracking

Lessons you create are public

This is a deliberate part of how TTL works, and we want to be completely upfront about it. When you generate a lesson or a series, the resulting subject, script, narration, and artwork are stored in a shared catalog that every user of the app can browse and play — through Charts, search, and recently-created lesson feeds. This is what lets everyone listen for free, including people who never generate a lesson themselves.

Your device's anonymous identifier (see above) is attached to what you create so the app can show it back to you under "Your Library" — but this identifier cannot be traced to your name, email, or Apple ID by us or by anyone else using the app. Nobody browsing Charts can see who created a given lesson.

You can delete anything you've created at any time from your Library. Deleting a lesson removes it from the shared catalog entirely — for everyone, not just from your own view of it. Uninstalling the app does not delete lessons you've created; they remain in the shared catalog until you explicitly delete them (from the same device, before uninstalling) or contact us to request removal.

Please don't include personal information in a lesson's subject — subjects are visible to every user of the app.

Playlists

Playlists you build are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded, synced, or visible to anyone else — including us. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes them.

Notifications

TTL can send an optional daily local reminder featuring a randomly selected lesson from the shared catalog. This is entirely opt-in, requested only when you turn it on in Settings, and can be turned off at any time in Settings or in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

Sharing

When you share a lesson or series, you're generating a link that leads either directly into the app (if the recipient has it installed) or to a simple preview page (if they don't). Anyone with that link can access that specific lesson — links aren't personal or traceable back to whoever shared them.

Ask a Question

If you tap "Ask a Question" after a lesson finishes, TTL requests microphone access to hear your spoken question. Your voice is transcribed to text using Apple's Speech framework, processed on your device wherever your device and language support that. On the small number of devices or languages where on-device transcription isn't available, Apple's own servers handle the transcription instead — TTL's own servers never receive your audio either way, only the transcribed text once that step is complete.

Only that transcribed text — never the audio itself — is sent to our secure proxy to generate a spoken answer in the same narrator voice. Neither your question nor the answer is saved anywhere by us; the whole exchange disappears once you leave the screen. This feature is entirely optional, only requests microphone access the first time you use it, and is only available with an active subscription.

How your lessons are generated

When you generate a lesson, your chosen subject is sent to our secure proxy, which uses AI services to generate the script and audio narration. These requests contain only your lesson topic and configuration — no personal information is included.

Advertising

Free plan users see banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device advertising identifiers to show relevant ads. You can limit ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Paid subscribers see no ads.

Analytics

We use Mixpanel, routed through the EU endpoint, to understand how the app is used in aggregate. All analytics are anonymised and tied only to your device identifier, never to a real-world identity. We do not use analytics to build personal profiles or identify who you are.

Data retention

Anonymous analytics events are retained for up to 12 months. Playlists and playback resume-position data live only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. Lessons you create remain in the shared catalog until you delete them yourself or request removal — uninstalling alone does not remove them, since they exist for other users to discover, not just for you.

Content moderation

Lessons may be automatically flagged as adult content based on their subject matter, and are visually marked as such wherever they appear in the app. We may remove content that violates our Terms of Service.

Your rights

Under applicable data protection law you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold. You can:

  • Delete any lesson or series you've created directly from your Library, at any time
  • Opt out of analytics by uninstalling the app
  • Turn off the daily notification at any time in Settings
  • Manage ad tracking in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Contact us with any data requests, including requesting removal of specific lesson content after uninstalling

Children

TTL is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. Given lessons are created by users and visible to everyone in the app, please don't create lessons intended for or directed at children with adult content, and be aware that the shared catalog reflects what other adult users have created.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do we will update the date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
